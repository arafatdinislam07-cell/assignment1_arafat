About Our Specialty Coffee Journey

Founded in 2018, our coffee roastery began as a passion project in a small garage. Over the past decade, we have expanded into a full-scale artisanal roastery serving local communities and bean enthusiasts worldwide. We pride ourselves on absolute transparency, uncompromised quality, and sustainable farm partnerships.

Key Historical Milestones

  1. 2018: First micro-batch roaster acquired
  2. 2020: Direct trade partnership launched in South America
  3. 2022: Flagship café opened in city center
  4. 2024: Voted Best Local Roastery
  5. 2026: Expanded fully sustainable zero-waste packaging

Interesting Coffee Facts

  • Coffee cherries are technically a fruit.
  • Light roasts contain slightly more caffeine than dark roasts.
  • Espresso contains less caffeine per serving than drip coffee.
  • Brazil produces the largest volume of coffee globally.
